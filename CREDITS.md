Almost every photograph on this site is the company's own: its vehicles, its
drivers, its yard. Those need no entry here.

This file records the exceptions, the stock photographs. Each one is listed
even where the licence does not ask for attribution, so that anyone later can
tell at a glance which images are ours and which are not. A stock photograph
must never be captioned or described as our fleet.
